Our story

Made in a small studio, not a factory.

Terra & Clay started in a converted barn with one wheel and one kiln. Every piece is still thrown by hand, trimmed the next day, and glazed with mixes we test ourselves — which means colors shift slightly from batch to batch.

We fire in small loads, so what's in stock is really what exists. When a piece sells out, it's gone until the next kiln load — usually 3 to 4 weeks.
